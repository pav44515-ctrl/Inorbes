Tracking Global Wealth

This dashboard aggregates data from Forbes, Bloomberg, and other financial sources to provide a comprehensive, daily-updated view of the world's wealthiest individuals. Net worth figures are calculated based on publicly available stock holdings, company valuations, and asset estimates.

Daily changes are simulated based on real market patterns and historical volatility. For official real-time data, visit Forbes Real-Time Billionaires or the Bloomberg Billionaires Index.
